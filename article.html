September Style Forecast: The Layering Pieces, Kimonos, and Jumpsuits You Need Right Now

11 September 2026·By marketing Shopzetu

Dear Zetu Fam,

September is here, and if the pattern holds, you already know what's coming: cool mornings that need a jacket, afternoons that don't, and zero patience for outfits that can't keep up. So, let's get your wardrobe together.

Our September style recommendations? Easy layers, statement kimonos, and one-and-done jumpsuits. Basically, pieces that do the thinking for you while still making people ask where you got it.

Layer Like You Mean It

Layering isn't about hiding the fit underneath, it's about giving it a little je ne sais quoi.

A structured blazer sharpens up monochrome instantly, while a relaxed cardigan brings texture to jeans, dresses, or your go-to tailored trousers.

Style tip: Choose layers you can shrug off and sling over your bag once the sun comes out. The best ones work just as well on their own as they do over an outfit.

The Kimono Is Doing Numbers

Want a simple fit to suddenly look styled? Throw a kimono over it.

From bold prints to fluid, floaty silhouettes, kimonos bring some personality to even your most basic pieces.

Wear it open over jeans and a tank for an easy daytime look, or let it add a little drama to a dress or jumpsuit.

The Jumpsuit: Zero Decisions, Full Slay

For the days that you want to look like you put in effort, without actually spending an hour deciding what to wear, the jumpsuit remains undefeated.

It's polished, versatile, and completely one-and-done. Keep it casual with flats and minimal accessories, or switch to heels and statement jewellery for dinner plans.

Your September Style Move

Stop shopping for gaps, start shopping for pieces that move with your plans! The ones that work for a Tuesday meeting and a Saturday hangout without a full wardrobe change.

Because September dressing shouldn't feel like a reset. It should feel like a really good excuse to remix what you already own.

Your style forecast? 100% chance of serving looks.
